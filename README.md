<h1 align="center">👩‍💼 HR Analysis Dashboard</h1>

<h2>📊 Overview</h2>
<p>
The <b>HR Analysis Dashboard</b> is an interactive data visualization project built using <b>Tableau</b>. 
It provides a comprehensive view of key human resource metrics such as employee demographics, attrition, performance, and department-wise statistics. 
This dashboard helps HR professionals and management make data-driven decisions for workforce planning and employee retention.
</p>

<hr>

<h2>🎯 Objectives</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Analyze <b>employee attrition rates</b> by age, gender, department, and job role.</li>
  <li>Track <b>employee performance</b> and satisfaction trends.</li>
  <li>Identify <b>factors influencing employee turnover</b>.</li>
  <li>Monitor <b>diversity, hiring trends, and experience levels</b> across departments.</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>⚙️ Tools & Technologies</h2>
<ul>
  <li><b>Tableau Public</b> – Used for building and publishing the interactive dashboard.</li>
  <li><b>Microsoft Excel / CSV</b> – Used for storing and cleaning HR data.</li>
  <li><b>Data Sources</b> – HR dataset from Kaggle or internal HR system exports.</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>📈 Key Insights</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Attrition rates are higher among employees with low job satisfaction and fewer years of experience.</li>
  <li>Most employees belong to the <b>Sales</b> and <b>Research & Development</b> departments.</li>
  <li>Correlation found between <b>salary hikes</b> and <b>employee retention</b>.</li>
  <li>Gender ratio analysis and its impact on department diversity.</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>💡 Features</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Interactive filters for <b>department</b>, <b>education</b>, <b>age group</b>, and <b>job role</b>.</li>
  <li>Dynamic KPIs for total employees, attrition %, and average satisfaction score.</li>
  <li>Trend lines showing <b>attrition over time</b> and <b>performance distribution</b>.</li>
  <li>Bar charts, heat maps, and donut charts for visual storytelling.</li>
</ul>
